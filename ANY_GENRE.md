# Any genre: how to ask for the story you want

[ภาษาไทย](ANY_GENRE.th.md) · [README](README.md)

Dungeons & Everything has no genre menu. At `/dne new` you describe the story you want in your own words, the Game Master reads it and pitches back how it will run it, and you change whatever you like. This guide shows how to write that description so you get the story you have in mind, and what each of the engine's genre presets gives you.

## How it works

1. You type `/dne new <name>` and describe the story. One line is enough; a full page is fine too.
2. The GM matches your description against 35 **genre presets**, and mixes them when your story is more than one thing. A preset is a bundle of small rules (a composure track for horror, heat after a loud job, seasons for a village), not a box your story has to fit. It takes up to five that are closest, the strongest first.
3. It pitches the result back in plain words: one line per rule and why it suits your story. No codes, no categories.
4. You say what to change. Then the GM builds the world, and a story generator rolls the bones of the plot so it does not repeat your last campaign.

Every new world runs on **Braid**, our own 2d6 rules made for any genre (see the [README](README.md#three-ways-to-play)), unless you ask for D&D 5e or for no dice at all.

## Mixing genres

The best stories are rarely one thing. Describe a story that is two or three things at once and the GM mixes up to five presets. Every rule from each one comes along: a slasher romance has a panic track and a doom track *and* strings and feelings between the two leads.

### Prompts that mix well

| Say | The GM reads | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| *"Summer camp, 1986. Two counselors are falling for each other while something picks off the campers."* | slasher + romance | panic, doom, supplies that run out; strings and feelings between the leads |
| *"We rob the Iron Sect's treasury during their tournament. One of us secretly works for them."* | heist + wuxia + traitor | position and effect, one plan then straight in, heat; cultivation realms, mooks, vows; secret roles under a seal |
| *"A quiet village where the lantern festival repeats every day, and every day someone is poisoned."* | mystery + time loop + cozy | clues that stay solvable, the loop, seasons and neighbours |
| *"A mining crew finds something in the ice. The air is running out, and so are our minds."* | cosmic horror + hard sci-fi | composure, clues, usage dice for air and parts, vows |
| *"A farming life inside a game world. The system grades every harvest."* | cozy + LitRPG | seasons, strings, a status window that announces everything |
| *"Two soldiers on opposite sides of the front, writing letters to each other."* | war + romance | the war layer, position and effect, rationed supplies; strings and feelings |
| *"The worst ghost-hunting channel in the country meets a real haunting."* | comedy + cosmic horror | mooks, composure, clues |
| *"A murder in the Ayutthaya palace, months before the city falls."* | historical + mystery | usage dice for what runs short, clues, a complication budget |
| *"A data heist on a corporate arcology in neon Bangkok."* | heist + space opera | position and effect, engagement, heat, mooks |
| *"A box of 1990s tapes holds the only clues to a disappearance."* | analog horror + mystery | composure, doom, clues |
| *"A cooking contest whose two finalists used to be married."* | competition + romance | contests, strings, feelings |
| *"A haunted rubber plantation in the deep south, and a death the police called an accident."* | Thai ghost story + mystery | composure, clues that stay solvable, a complication budget |
| *"Two spies from rival agencies, posing as a married couple in Hanoi, slowly stop pretending."* | spy + romance | position and effect, heat, a sealed double agent; strings and feelings |
| *"Plane crash on a jungle mountain. Six survivors, and one of them caused it."* | survival + traitor | usage dice, doom, a vow to get home, secret roles under a seal |
| *"Four kids in a 1990s provincial town, and the cinema that shows a film nobody made."* | kids' adventure + cosmic horror | identity labels, strings, clues, composure |

### How the mix is decided

- **The first genre leads.** It sets the narration's voice, the pacing and the difficulty. *Slasher + romance* is a horror story with a love story inside it (dread, constant pressure, deadly); *romance + slasher* is a love story that turns into horror (banter, slow, easy until it isn't). Say which one is the main story and the GM orders them that way.
- **Clashes are your call.** Every place the genres disagree is named, never settled silently. The GM asks in story words: *"constant pressure like the slasher, or slow like the romance?"*, *"can we invent details, or only what the world supports?"*
- **One plot drives.** Two plot-driving stories (*a heist and a murder*) need one in front; the GM asks *"is this mainly the heist, or mainly the murder?"* and the other becomes a thread inside it.
- **Eras stay in their era, unless you say otherwise.** Western, pirates, fantasy, wuxia and historical drop the other genres' modern things (radios, phones, cars). Want them mixed on purpose, like pirates with smartphones or a cyber-western? Say so (*"pirates, but in today's world"*) and the GM keeps everything.
- **Up to five genres.** More than eight rules at once is allowed, but the GM will offer to trim.
- **Change anything after.** *"Keep the slasher, drop the feelings rule."* *"Add a secret traitor."*

### Writing a mixed prompt

- Put both sides in one sentence: *"a heist, with a slow romance between the safecracker and the inside man"*.
- Say which side is the main story, or let the order of your words say it.
- Say what the second genre adds: *"a war story, but what matters is the letters"*.
- Odd mixes are welcome. *Cozy + slasher*, *wuxia + courtroom*, *LitRPG + time loop*: if the rules clash, the GM asks, and you pick.

## Writing a good prompt

The GM can work from one word (*"cyberpunk"*), but each of these you add makes the story more yours. None is required.

| Say something about | Why it matters | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **What kind of story** it is | picks the presets and their rules | *a murder case*, *a slow love story*, *a heist that goes wrong* |
| **The world and the era** | the setting, the threats, the gear | *a fishing village in 1970s Thailand*, *a generation ship 300 years out* |
| **Who you are** | the characters and why they are together | *three cousins home for a funeral*, *a crew of thieves who owe the same man* |
| **How it opens** | the first scene | *the night the lighthouse goes dark* |
| **What you want to feel** | the narration's voice and pace | *dread that builds slowly*, *fast and funny*, *quiet and warm* |
| **What you want to do a lot of** | where the rules put their weight | *talking people round*, *planning*, *running*, *fighting* |
| **What you do not want** | lines and veils, content level | *no gore*, *no harm to animals*, *romance off-screen* |
| **How hard and how long** | difficulty and the size of the arc | *brutal, characters can die*, *a one-shot*, *a long campaign* |

A template you can copy:

```
I want a [kind of story] set in [world / era]. We play [who we are].
It starts when [the opening]. I want it to feel [feel]. We'll mostly [what we do].
Please no [what to avoid]. Make it [easy / hard / deadly], [one-shot / long campaign].
```

**Weak:** *"horror"* — the GM will ask more questions, and guess the rest.

**Strong:** *"Horror in a Thai fishing village during the monsoon. We are three cousins back for our grandmother's funeral. Something lives in the fog and the village won't talk about it. Slow dread, not jump scares. A mystery we can actually solve. No gore."* — the GM can read cosmic horror plus a fair mystery, slow pacing, a hard difficulty and a content limit straight from it.

**Facts you state are kept.** *"The killer must be the abbot"*, *"the vault is under the old cinema"*: the GM pins these in the story generator, and its check fails if anything later contradicts them. Anything you leave open, it rolls.

## Changing the pitch

After the pitch, just say what you want different. Some useful phrases:

| You say | What changes |
|---|---|
| *"No sanity track"*, *"drop the supplies thing"* | that rule is switched off |
| *"Add a time limit"*, *"make it a race against the clock"* | a doom track or a countdown |
| *"Slower, more talking"* / *"constant pressure"* | `pacing`: `slow` / `adventure` |
| *"Make it brutal"* / *"we're new, go easy"* | `difficulty`: `deadly` / `easy` |
| *"Let us invent details"* / *"only what the world supports"* | `claims`: `open` / `grounded` (`declare`: small details just become true, big ones cost a token) |
| *"Noir voice"*, *"pulp action"*, *"lots of banter"*, *"creeping dread"* | the narration's voice (`storyteller`) |
| *"We're all adults, go harder"* | content `18` or `20` (that is you confirming everyone's age) |
| *"Romance can happen but fade to black"* | romance `fade` (`off`, `fade` or `open`) |
| *"No dice at all"* | story mode: an oracle answers every risk |
| *"Use D&D 5e rules"* | the 5e engine instead of Braid |

You can change modules between sessions too; the GM notes it in the session log. The Table Settings page (`setup.html`) sets the dials with ticks instead of typing.

## The genre presets

Each preset below lists what it plays like, words that point the GM at it, two example prompts, the rules it brings and the defaults it sets. **Early** presets run on rules of our own design with the least play behind them. Every default can be changed.

### Mystery

A case with one true answer, built so you cannot get stuck.

- **Say things like:** detective, murder, whodunit, noir, cold case, police, *who did it*.
- **Try:** *"A noir murder in 1950s Bangkok. I'm a disgraced police captain; my partner is a reporter. The victim is a nightclub singer and everyone lies."* · *"A locked-room death at a mountain monastery. We're pilgrims snowed in with the killer."*
- **Rules:** **clues**: the answer is fixed before play and checked so the case stays solvable even if you miss a third of the clues; **a complication budget**: the GM can bring in a few unasked troubles per session, and no more. Optional: *make the case* (no hidden answer: a well-argued theory becomes true), composure, secret roles.
- **Defaults:** noir voice, mixed pacing, standard difficulty.

### Heist

A crew, a target, a plan that will not survive contact.

- **Say things like:** heist, job, crew, vault, con, caper, smuggling, *the big score*.
- **Try:** *"A heist on a floating casino in the Gulf of Thailand. Five thieves, one night, and the owner is someone's ex."* · *"A crew of retired con artists pull one last job on the man who ruined them."*
- **Rules:** **position and effect**: before a risky roll the GM says how bad a miss gets and how much a success gains; **engagement**: pick one plan and one detail, then go straight in (flashbacks cover what you "had prepared"); **heat**: crew heat and *wanted* rise after a loud job. Optional: a complication budget, secret roles (one of you is a plant), contests.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty; `declare` claims so you can say *"I bribed the guard yesterday"*.

### War

The war is bigger than you.

- **Say things like:** soldiers, the front, resistance, siege, a unit, orders.
- **Try:** *"A resistance cell in an occupied city, 1943. We smuggle people out and every week the net closes."* · *"A squad of conscripts on a moon war nobody back home remembers."*
- **Rules:** **a war layer**: four party tracks (time, pressure, morale, supply) that move between missions; position and effect; **usage dice** for ammunition, medicine and rations (they run out unevenly, not by counting). Optional: engagement, mooks, secret roles, a complication budget.
- **Defaults:** plain voice, constant pressure, deadly, content suggested at 18.

### Competition / courtroom *(early)*

Two sides want the same thing, over several rounds.

- **Say things like:** tournament, race, trial, court, game show, sports, contest, election.
- **Try:** *"A street-food competition in Yaowarat. Our family stall against a celebrity chef; three rounds, one week."* · *"A courtroom drama: we defend a maid accused of killing her employer."*
- **Rules:** **contests**: races, countdowns and concessions, scored round by round from ordinary rolls. Optional: clues, strings, a complication budget.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, mixed pacing, standard difficulty.

### Cosmic horror

Small wrong details, things that should not be, knowledge that costs.

- **Say things like:** horror, haunting, cult, the occult, an ancient god, something unknowable.
- **Try:** *"A cult on a small island is waiting for something under the sea, and the ferry only comes on Sundays."* · *"Researchers at an Antarctic station dig up a signal older than the ice."*
- **Rules:** **composure**: a 0–10 mind track that only goes down; seeing what should not be costs it; **clues**. Optional: doom, a time loop, memories.
- **Defaults:** dread voice, slow pacing, hard difficulty, content suggested at 18.

### Slasher / survival horror

Something is hunting you and the night is long.

- **Say things like:** being hunted, a killer, zombies, a creature, an outbreak, a home invasion.
- **Try:** *"Six friends at a lake house. The ferry doesn't come back. Something in the woods is picking us off."* · *"A zombie outbreak in a Bangkok condo tower; we're on the 31st floor and the lifts are dead."*
- **Rules:** **panic**: fear piles onto stress and the script rolls what you do when you break; **doom**: a party track that rises with every risk and brings disasters; **usage dice** for batteries, fuel, the last shell. Optional: a complication budget, mooks.
- **Defaults:** dread voice, constant pressure, deadly, content suggested at 18.

### Analog horror *(early)*

The horror is in the recording.

- **Say things like:** creepypasta, found footage, VHS, lost broadcast, tapes, liminal spaces, an archive of anomalies.
- **Try:** *"We find a box of 1990s Thai TV tapes. The children's show on tape 4 has a segment that never aired."* · *"Night-shift staff at a mall that has more floors on the camera feed than in the building."*
- **Rules:** composure and doom, told through tapes, transcripts and broadcasts; evidence that changes when you look again. Optional: clues, panic.
- **Defaults:** dread voice, slow pacing, hard difficulty, content suggested at 18.

### Romance / drama

The stakes are other people.

- **Say things like:** love story, drama, family, rivals to lovers, high school, office, soap opera.
- **Try:** *"Two rival noodle shops on the same soi; their heirs keep ending up together."* · *"A family drama: four siblings, one inheritance, a house in Chiang Mai."*
- **Rules:** **strings**: holds one character has on another (a secret known, a promise owed); **emotions**: a feeling that weighs on one approach until you act on it. Optional: identity labels, memories, vows.
- **Defaults:** banter voice, slow pacing, easy difficulty, `declare` claims; romance suggested at `fade`.

### Cozy / slice of life

Nobody has to fight. Trouble is a problem to solve together.

- **Say things like:** cozy, ordinary life, village, café, shop, farm, found family, *nothing bad happens*.
- **Try:** *"A year running a small café by the river. Each season brings new regulars and small problems."* · *"Retired witches running a bookshop. The worst thing is the landlord."*
- **Rules:** **seasons** that turn with the calendar and bring small needs; **strings** with the neighbours. No harm track. Optional: *make the case*, contests, emotions.
- **Defaults:** banter voice, slow pacing, easy, `open` claims (you co-write the village).

### Comedy / gonzo

It escalates.

- **Say things like:** comedy, sitcom, parody, satire, absurd, chaos, slapstick.
- **Try:** *"We are the worst ghost-hunting YouTube channel in Thailand and tonight a real ghost watches the stream."* · *"Office comedy at a space station's HR department."*
- **Rules:** **mooks**: nameless foes that come and go down in groups. Optional: contests, *make the case*, emotions, labels, a status window.
- **Defaults:** banter voice, constant pressure, easy, `open` claims.

### Fantasy adventure

Roads, ruins and old oaths.

- **Say things like:** fantasy, magic, quest, ruins, kingdoms, fairy tale, myth, folklore.
- **Try:** *"A quest through the Himmapan forest to return a stolen naga pearl before the rains."* · *"Sword and sorcery: two mercenaries, one cursed sword, a city that wants both."*
- **Rules:** **vows**: promises tracked over many sessions; **usage dice** for torches, food and rope. Optional: mooks, seasons, memories, realms.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty. (For classic D&D with classes and spells, ask for 5e.)

### Hard science fiction

Distance, air and physics are the danger.

- **Say things like:** sci-fi, space, first contact, aliens, colony, generation ship.
- **Try:** *"First contact on a mining asteroid. The thing in the ice is answering our drills."* · *"A colony on Mars, year 12. The supply ship is late and the council is lying about why."*
- **Rules:** **usage dice** for air, fuel and parts; **vows**. Optional: panic, a status window, position and effect.
- **Defaults:** plain voice, mixed pacing, hard difficulty.

### Space opera / cyberpunk

Chrome, corps and crews on the run.

- **Say things like:** space opera, cyberpunk, megacity, corporation, starship, rebellion, space pirates.
- **Try:** *"Neon Bangkok 2077. We're couriers for a data broker; tonight's package is a person."* · *"A smuggler crew with a ship older than all of us and a debt to a syndicate."*
- **Rules:** position and effect, **heat**, **mooks**. Optional: engagement, labels, vows.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty.

### Wuxia / cultivation *(early)*

Sects, grudges, and the long climb to power.

- **Say things like:** wuxia, xianxia, kung fu, cultivation, sect, jianghu, revenge.
- **Try:** *"An outer disciple of a fallen sect, framed for the master's death. I want to cultivate and clear my name."* · *"Wandering swordsmen escort a treasure every sect wants."*
- **Rules:** **realms**: cultivation stages that decide what you can do at all, with tribulations to break through; mooks; vows. Optional: memories, contests.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty, `declare` claims.

### LitRPG / another world *(early)*

The world has a system, and it talks.

- **Say things like:** LitRPG, isekai, trapped in a game, levels, loot, dungeon tower, system.
- **Try:** *"We wake up inside the MMO we played in high school. The tutorial NPC remembers us."* · *"A tower appears over the city; every floor cleared gives the survivors a new skill."*
- **Rules:** **a status window**: the script announces every change in system messages; mooks. Optional: vows, usage dice, realms.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty.

### Historical / docudrama

The real past, where institutions act and wounds linger.

- **Say things like:** historical, documentary, true story, period drama, a real era, alternate history.
- **Try:** *"Ayutthaya, 1767, the last months before the fall. We are palace clerks who know too much."* · *"A documentary feel: a 1980s newsroom covering a cover-up."*
- **Rules:** **usage dice** for what runs short; wounds are conditions, not numbers. Optional: seasons, a war layer, vows, composure.
- **Defaults:** plain voice, slow pacing, hard difficulty.

### Time loop

The same day again. The world forgets; you do not.

- **Say things like:** time loop, the same day, it resets, living it over and over, fixing the past.
- **Try:** *"Every time the school bell rings at 4 pm the day restarts. Someone in our class dies at 3:55."* · *"A wedding day that loops until we find out who poisons the groom."*
- **Rules:** **the loop**: the engine rewinds the world to the morning and keeps what the characters learned; something changes each time. Optional: memories, clues.
- **Defaults:** plain voice, mixed pacing, standard difficulty.

### Traitor among us

One of you is not who they say.

- **Say things like:** traitor, mole, social deduction, werewolf or mafia game, *who can we trust*.
- **Try:** *"An expedition crew on a stranded ship. One of us is working for the company that left us here."* · *"A resistance cell. Someone keeps tipping off the police."*
- **Rules:** **secret roles**: the script deals roles under a seal, nobody (not even the GM) can move the traitor afterwards, and anyone can check the seal at the end. Optional: clues, a complication budget.
- **Defaults:** noir voice, mixed pacing, standard difficulty.

### Survival *(early)*

No villain. The land, the weather and the group are the danger.

- **Say things like:** survival, stranded, castaway, crash, flood, earthquake, lost at sea, a trek, *no help is coming*.
- **Try:** *"Our tour bus went off a mountain road in the rainy season. No signal, the river is rising, and we have to get down on our own."* · *"We're the only survivors of a boat that sank in the Andaman Sea, washed up on an island with no people. Water and food are almost gone."*
- **Rules:** **usage dice** for food, water and fuel; **doom**: the weather and the land close in with every risk; **vows**: the promise to get everyone home. Optional: panic, seasons, a complication budget.
- **Defaults:** plain voice, constant pressure, hard difficulty.

### Thai ghost story / folk horror *(early)*

Spirits with a reason to stay, and a village that knows more than it says.

- **Say things like:** ghost, phi pop, krasue, spirit house, a curse, a broken vow, karma, a haunted village, folk horror.
- **Try:** *"I go back to my grandmother's village in Isan for her funeral, and people say there's a phi pop. Everyone's scared, but nobody will say whose body it's in."* · *"We just moved into a condo. They tore down the old spirit house to build it; now strange things happen every night, and the old guard says this used to be a graveyard."*
- **Rules:** **composure**; **clues**: the wrong that keeps the spirit here can always be found (a mystery thread is added to build that case). The story generator rolls the spirit, the wrong never set right, the taboo broken, who still knows the old ways, and the rite that could put it to rest. Optional: memories, doom, emotions.
- **Defaults:** dread voice, slow pacing, hard difficulty, content suggested at 18.

### Psychological thriller / twist *(early)*

Someone close is lying, and your own memory is not on your side.

- **Say things like:** thriller, twist, unreliable narrator, lost memory, gaslighting, a missing person, *I can't trust myself*.
- **Try:** *"I wake up in hospital with no memory of that night. My husband says I crashed the car on my own, but there's a text on my phone I don't remember sending."* · *"My sister went missing a week ago. Everyone says she ran away. I'm sure someone is lying, and the more I dig, the less sure I am of myself."*
- **Rules:** **clues**; **memories**: a mind holds only five, and what it lets go is gone; **a complication budget**. The twist is rolled (or pinned by you) before play, with a mystery thread for the case. Optional: composure, secret roles, strings.
- **Defaults:** noir voice, mixed pacing, hard difficulty.

### Spy / espionage *(early)*

Covers, assets, and a handler who may not be on your side.

- **Say things like:** spy, agent, undercover, double agent, defection, handler, extraction, cold war.
- **Try:** *"We're spies posing as hotel staff in Bangkok. The job is to steal a minister's bribe ledger before the summit, but the handler thinks one of us is a double agent."* · *"Get a defecting scientist across the Mekong before dawn while the other side's agents close in."*
- **Rules:** position and effect; **heat**: every loud move draws attention; **secret roles**: the double agent is dealt under a seal. Optional: engagement, strings, clues.
- **Defaults:** noir voice, mixed pacing, standard difficulty, `declare` claims (*"I planted a contact there last year"*).

### Post-apocalypse *(early)*

After the end: what is left, and what is worth rebuilding.

- **Say things like:** post-apocalypse, wasteland, after the end, survivors, scavenging, a settlement, rebuilding.
- **Try:** *"Ten years after the grid died, we run a walled-in school and the well is going bad."* · *"We scavenge the drowned city for medicine and have to deal with the river gangs."*
- **Rules:** **usage dice** for what is scarce; **seasons**: winters and harvests set the year; **vows** to rebuild. Optional: a war layer, mooks, panic.
- **Defaults:** plain voice, mixed pacing, hard difficulty.

### Superheroes *(early)*

Powers with a price, a city that is not sure it wants you, and a secret identity.

- **Say things like:** superhero, powers, vigilante, a hero team, a villain, a secret identity.
- **Try:** *"I'm a college student with powers, and every time I use them I lose a memory."* · *"A hero team in Bangkok that the city blames for the last flood."*
- **Rules:** **identity labels**: who you are in your own eyes and the city's, shifting with what you do; **mooks** to throw around; **strings** with the people who know your secret. Optional: emotions, heat, vows.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty, `declare` claims.

### Western / frontier *(early)*

Dust, water rights, outlaws and the railroad coming.

- **Say things like:** western, frontier, cowboy, outlaw, sheriff, gunfight, ranch, railroad town.
- **Try:** *"A dusty frontier town. A cattle baron owns the water, and the railroad is coming."* · *"An old outlaw trying to clear his name before the showdown at noon."*
- **Rules:** **usage dice** for bullets, water and feed; **vows**; **heat**: the law and the bounty hunters. Optional: mooks, contests, strings. Mixed with other genres, the era drops what it cannot have (radios, phones, cars, recordings).
- **Defaults:** plain voice, constant pressure, hard difficulty.

### Kids' adventure / coming of age *(early)*

A summer, a small town, and a secret the grown-ups won't see.

- **Say things like:** kids, teens, summer break, a gang of friends, a small town, growing up, *the adults don't believe us*.
- **Try:** *"A gang of kids in a small town over summer break finds something in the woods the grown-ups won't believe."* · *"Teenagers in an Isan railway town, the last summer before one of us moves away."*
- **Rules:** **identity labels** for who each kid is becoming; **strings** between friends; **clues** to the secret (a mystery thread builds it). Optional: emotions, composure, seasons.
- **Defaults:** banter voice, mixed pacing, standard difficulty, `declare` claims.

### Court intrigue / politics *(early)*

The throne, the council, the vote: won with favours, secrets and poison.

- **Say things like:** court, palace, succession, heir, council, scheming nobles, a royal marriage, an election.
- **Try:** *"A dying king, three heirs, and every one of us owes a favour to someone at court. We have until the coronation."* · *"I play a lady-in-waiting who knows who poisoned the queen, and has to decide who to sell that secret to."*
- **Rules:** **strings**: favours and secrets held over each other; **secret roles**: who is really loyal to whom; **a complication budget**. Optional: emotions, clues, *make the case*.
- **Defaults:** noir voice, slow pacing, standard difficulty.

### Pirates / high seas *(early)*

A ship, a crew that might mutiny, and something worth sailing for.

- **Say things like:** pirates, ship, crew, captain, treasure, smugglers, navy, mutiny, the high seas.
- **Try:** *"A ragged pirate crew with a stolen sloop and half a treasure map, and a navy frigate two days behind us."* · *"Smugglers in the Andaman Sea in monsoon season: storms, ports full of spies, and a captain nobody trusts."*
- **Rules:** **usage dice** for water, powder and rations; **vows**; **mooks** for boarding fights. Optional: heat, contests, seasons. Mixed with other genres, the era drops cars, radios, phones and railways.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty, `declare` claims.

### Kaiju / giant robots *(early)*

Something enormous comes out of the sea, and the city is in between.

- **Say things like:** giant monster, kaiju, giant robot, mecha, pilot, a city under attack.
- **Try:** *"A giant monster keeps rising out of the gulf, and we pilot the one machine that can still fight it."* · *"Bangkok is flooding because something huge is moving in the river, and we have to get the city out alive."*
- **Rules:** **a war layer**: time, pressure, morale and supply between attacks; **mooks**; **doom**: every attack brings the city closer to the end. Optional: position and effect, strings, usage dice.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, hard difficulty.

### Crime family / gangster *(early)*

Family, territory, loyalty, and the law closing in.

- **Say things like:** mafia, gangster, crime family, the mob, turf war, the boss, an informant.
- **Try:** *"Our family runs the docks and the underground lottery. The old boss just died, and someone in the family is talking to the police."* · *"A rival family is moving on our night market, and there's a debt nobody will forgive."*
- **Rules:** **heat** from the police and rivals; **strings**: loyalty and debts inside the family; **secret roles**: the informant is dealt under a seal. Optional: position and effect, a complication budget, emotions.
- **Defaults:** noir voice, mixed pacing, hard difficulty, `declare` claims, content suggested at 18.

### Portal fantasy *(early)*

Ordinary people cross into another world, owe something on arrival, and have to choose which world is home.

- **Say things like:** another world, a doorway, summoned, stranded, crossing over, *we want to get home* (with no game system: for levels and status screens, see LitRPG).
- **Try:** *"Three office workers take the last night bus and get off at a second dawn, in a town that packs its houses onto sleds."* · *"A family's old wardrobe opens onto a salt orchard, and the people there think we owe them for the fruit we ate."*
- **Rules:** **vows**: the promise to get home (or to stay); position and effect. Optional: emotions, realms, a status window.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, standard difficulty, `declare` claims.

### Road trip *(early)*

The journey is the story: stops, detours, and what the travelers say to each other on the way.

- **Say things like:** road trip, one last drive, a journey, a tour, a pilgrimage, stops along the way.
- **Try:** *"Four old classmates drive from Bangkok to Mae Hong Son for a friend's last exhibition, one stop a session."* · *"A touring band on a tiny budget, ten gigs in ten towns, and a van that might not make it."*
- **Rules:** **vows**: the promise to arrive; **emotions**: what is left unsaid weighs on the roll until it is said. Optional: usage dice for fuel and money, a complication budget, contests.
- **Defaults:** plain voice, mixed pacing, easy difficulty.

### Abyssal delve *(early)*

A chasm with layers, a strange ecology, and getting back up is the hard part.

- **Say things like:** megadungeon, delve, the depths, expedition, relic hunters, camping underground, *deeper layers get stranger*.
- **Try:** *"Relic hunters from a rim town go down the great sinkhole to bring back a sister's compass from the fourth tier."* · *"An expedition into a cave system under Isan, where the deeper you go, the slower time runs."*
- **Rules:** **usage dice** for lamp oil, rope and food; **doom**: every risk in the deep draws something closer; position and effect. Optional: mooks, vows, a complication budget.
- **Defaults:** pulp voice, constant pressure, hard difficulty.

### Urban occult / night world *(early)*

The city has a second life after dark: clans, exorcists, and a truce nobody fully trusts.

- **Say things like:** vampires, night clans, exorcists, a secret supernatural world, a truce, blood debts, *the mortals mustn't know*.
- **Try:** *"Night-shift exorcists in Bangkok, and someone is selling cursed amulets at the Chatuchak night market."* · *"A neutral-ground jazz club, three rival families, and a regent burned to ash in a locked lift."*
- **Rules:** **strings**: favours and blood debts; **heat**: every loud night draws the police and the clans; **emotions**. Optional: doom, mooks, vows.
- **Defaults:** noir voice, mixed pacing, hard difficulty, content suggested at 18, romance suggested at `fade`.

### Magic academy *(early)*

Classes, houses, curfew, and something old behind the classroom wall.

- **Say things like:** magic school, academy, students, houses, exams, curfew, a secret society on campus.
- **Try:** *"First year at a magic school built on a storm cloud. The top graduate vanished during last year's final exam."* · *"Scholarship students at a school of talisman calligraphy, and someone is selling exam charms that eat memories."*
- **Rules:** **identity labels**: who each student is becoming; **strings** with rivals and friends; **seasons**: the term turns and brings exams and holidays. Optional: clues, contests, emotions.
- **Defaults:** banter voice, mixed pacing, standard difficulty, `declare` claims, romance suggested at `fade`.

## When nothing fits

A description no preset matches still works. The GM starts from the nearest preset and swaps rules, or uses plain Braid with only the rules your story needs. Some examples:

- *"A cooking show where we are the contestants and the judges"*: competition's contests with cozy's easy difficulty and `open` claims.
- *"An ordinary year in an ordinary family"*: cozy's seasons and strings, nothing else.
- *"A mockumentary about our office"*: comedy with `open` claims and no fights; the camera crew is part of the joke.
- *"A documentary about our own town"*: historical, low danger, `open` claims so you bring the stories.

If you are not sure what is possible, ask the GM *"what kind of stories could we play?"*. It answers with example stories, not a list.

## A different story every time

The story generator rolls the skeleton of each new story (for a mystery: the crime, the victim, who did it, why, how, the twist, the red herring) from each genre's own lists, and remembers every past campaign so the next one differs. A mystery is checked before play: every place reachable, every conclusion backed by at least three clues in at least two places, and still solvable in 95% of 1000 trials that hide a third of the clues.

Pin anything you want fixed (*"the culprit is the lighthouse keeper"*); leave the rest open and it surprises you too.
