# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Groups playing a tabletop RPG run by an AI Game Master. Two equal ways to play: everyone around one
shared screen (TV or laptop) with each player sending actions from their own phone, or one person at a
laptop reading and typing on the same screen. Players are often Thai; text mixes Thai and English.

## Product Purpose
The live display shows the story as the GM writes it and lets players say what their characters do.
Success: reading is effortless at any distance, and sending an action feels as quick as a chat message.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, readable, familiar. The story is the show; the interface steps back.

## Anti-references
Theatrical fantasy chrome (parchment, glows, all-caps tracked labels) as the default; game-HUD clutter;
anything that breaks Thai shaping (letter-spacing on Thai text).

## Design Principles
- Story first: chrome stays out of the reading column.
- Chat conventions for input: composer at the bottom, Enter to send on keyboards, state shown inline.
- One vocabulary across the shared screen and the phone controller.
- Thai is a first-class script: fonts, line height, no tracking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in light and dark; 40px+ touch targets on phones; respects reduced motion.
No extra requirements stated by the owner.
